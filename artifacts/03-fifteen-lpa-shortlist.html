What actually moves you from 10 to 15+

  1. Lead with the agentic AI work, not the analytics. Every analytics fresher has SQL, Power BI and an EDA project. Almost none have shipped 10 stateful LLM agents in parallel and cut latency 7×, or built citation-grounded RAG over a statute corpus. That is the 25–45% premium skill set, and it is the reason a product company or AI-native startup would pay you 18 rather than 10. Your resume currently buries it under the internships — reorder it.
  2. Use the freelance revenue as experience, not as a hobby line. Rs 6L+ delivered across 10+ commercial projects, and a platform shipped in 8 days for 10,000+ users, reads as ownership. Combined with four internships that is a defensible 16–20 months of professional work, which clears the "0–2 years" band that most of these roles actually screen on.
  3. Referrals are worth most where the portal is a black hole — product companies and AI-native startups, where a hiring manager reading your GitHub in five minutes beats an ATS keyword match. They are worth least at IT services and Big-4, where the process is volume-driven anyway. Spend your connections on the first group.
  4. The two-to-six week gap-closers that unlock the most postings: Tableau Desktop basics (your Power BI transfers; dozens of reqs say "Tableau or similar"), SQL window functions and CTEs, one cloud fundamentals cert (AWS Cloud Practitioner or Azure AI-900), and Databricks/Snowflake fundamentals. None of these is Java or C++, and each one is named explicitly in reqs you would otherwise be filtered out of.
