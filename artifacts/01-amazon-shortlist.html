How this list was built

  1. Pulled all 2,339 live India postings from the amazon.jobs search API, each with its full basic and preferred qualifications text.
  2. Parsed every qualification bullet for a stated experience threshold, in years or months. Bullets are ANDed, so a posting's real bar is its highest requirement — that is what the gauge shows.
  3. Kept roles at ≤ 1 year, dropped manager and senior-titled reqs, then required the qualifications to name a skill you hold (SQL, Python, Excel, PowerBI, analytics, ML/GenAI).
  4. Ranked what survived by how directly the posted wording maps to what is on your resume.
