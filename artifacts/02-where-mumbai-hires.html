Where Mumbai actually hires

Thirty employers big enough to run Amazon-scale intake, filtered for the ones that move fast and hire your profile. But first, a finding that should change your search: I pulled live opening counts straight from seven companies' careers systems, and Mumbai is not a product-tech city.

Live openings today, Mumbai vs rest of India

Queried directly from each company's careers API on 24 Sep 2026 — not scraped from a jobs aggregator. The split is the whole argument.

Mumbai Rest of India

Read the top three rows against the bottom three. The banks and the analytics firm put most of their India hiring in Mumbai. The product-tech companies put almost none of it there — NVIDIA has 199 roles in Bengaluru and 13 in Mumbai; S&P Global has 13 in Hyderabad and 1 in Mumbai. So the honest answer to your question splits in two: if you stay in Mumbai, your volume is BFSI captives, analytics pure-plays and consulting. If you want product-tech scale, that is a Bengaluru, Hyderabad or Pune decision, and worth making deliberately rather than by accident.

Two things before you start working through these

  • Your campus is the cheat code, and it expires. DJ Sanghvi is a target school for TCS, Accenture, Deloitte, LTIMindtree, Fractal and ZS. A campus process converts in weeks and skips the resume filter that just blocked you at Amazon. You are in your final year — this channel is open now and gone after June 2027. Work it before, not after, the off-campus list.
  • "Fast" here means hiring model, not a measured stat. I rated speed on how each company hires: cohort and volume recruiters (IT services, analytics pure-plays, BPM) run batched drives and close in two to four weeks; BFSI captives and consulting run four to eight weeks with more rounds; Big Tech runs headcount-approval hiring that stalls for months, which is exactly what you hit with Microsoft. Treat the pill as a planning estimate, not a promise.
