How to read this

Match
How closely the posted requirements line up with the resume. It says nothing about pay or how good the job is.
Chance
Rough likelihood of getting through, built from the stated experience bar, whether it is an internship, and how far the requirements sit above ~16–20 months of experience. A planning estimate, not a promise.
Pay
Indicative India CTC band for that employer at that level, from public salary reporting. Not an offer, and internships pay a stipend rather than a CTC.
Verified
Read in full, screened against the resume, then adversarially re-checked with the link tested against the employer's own hiring API.
Unscreened
Found in a live crawl and kept because it looked relevant. Read the posting before applying.

Asking for a referral

Open a row and use “Find a referrer”. Order: DJSCE alumni first, then people in the named team, then recruiters. Send a connection note (under 300 characters), and only ask for the referral once they reply. Always include the job ID.

Hi [Name], I'm Omkar, a final-year CSE (Data Science) student at DJSCE graduating May 2027. I've done
fintech analytics at CypherSOL (loan-eligibility / bank-statement work), a PM internship at Bigul and
founder's office at Infiheal. I'm applying for [Role, Job ID] at [Company]. Would you be open to
referring me? Happy to send my resume and a two-line summary. Thank you!

Availability line to use everywhere: graduating May 2027, available full-time from June 2027; available for a full-time internship from January to May 2027.
